Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished landscape 3D game environment design sheet for "03 蒼壁峽灣" (Azure Cliff Fjord), a BUILDABLE maritime castle town region for an existing stylized low-poly pirate sailing game. This is an actionable environment concept, not a photorealistic fantasy painting. Prior discussed references: Mediterranean cliffside castles with cream limestone walls and muted terracotta roof accents, massive layered fortifications rising from irregular grey-blue sea cliffs; a dense stepped harbor town; tall navigable sea gate and bridges. Translate these into restrained modular game art with believable construction.

Composition: large detailed three-quarter bird's eye orthographic-like overview occupies left 75%, south/front sea approach faces viewer, north/back elevated castle. Small clean TOP-DOWN LAYOUT inset in upper right, with matching land/water layout and north arrow. Small scale silhouettes of the SAME 6m-long sailing ship near the entrance and harbor, not oversized. Lower right has concise neat numbered legend. Title top left exactly "03 蒼壁峽灣" and subtitle "海上城堡・階梯港鎮". Warm daylight, clear blue water and turquoise coastal shallows; entire regional footprint visible, neutral blue sea background, no distant mountains.

Concrete proposed layout: asymmetric rugged horseshoe headland, OPEN SOUTH harbor, NOT circular donut. Approximate regional footprint 120m wide x 100m deep as a design target. A highest rear-north rock terrace carries one coherent dominant castle: one square main keep, four unequal secondary towers, connecting inhabited halls and two stepped defensive wall terraces following the rock. Largest keep roof reaches roughly 42m above water, supporting terrain tops 20m; towers are finite simple masses, no forests of spires. 16-20 small distinct attached/closely spaced town buildings in two terraced neighborhoods on left/west and right/east harbor arms below castle, every foundation seated on rock, retaining walls and stairs join levels. Buildings use 4-6 reusable families: narrow townhouse, wider inn, warehouse, workshop, small watchtower. Cream grey masonry, teal-grey shutters and select dull red tiled roofs. Greenery only in gardens/rock ledges, never covers navigation.

Main central harbor basin approximately 40m clear turning diameter, kept OPEN WATER, four simple grounded timber/stone quay fingers along sides with obvious shore access. South entrance has a SINGLE functional monumental sea-gate arch linking TWO short rock piers/bridgeheads, with about 20m horizontal clearance and 14m clear height ABOVE water under arch, opening clearly shows continuous water into harbor; avoid a solid wall blocking channel. A secondary EAST side natural channel about 14m wide remains open to sea and joins same inner basin; an elevated short stone footbridge crosses it with generous sailing clearance. Gate is subordinate in height to main castle and has physically supported voussoir arch masonry, normal thickness not a paper ribbon. A low detached southwest watchtower rock with shallow fringe, no decorative rocks in navigation. Irregular cliffs variable heights with shelves, not repeated vertical cylinders, no symmetrical perimeter, no floating islands, no disconnected stair flights, no protruding unattached beams, no roof/wall gaps, no implausible gravity defiance.

Buildability: clearly readable large polygon surfaces, simple chamfered masses, restrained details suited to modular meshes under ~5000 triangles each (not whole scene). Stone blocks suggested by subtle sparse material pattern, not millions of sculpted bricks. Geometry silhouette and functional connections take priority over tiny ornamental detail. Preserve visible navigable lanes, no dense parked ships. Diagrams must match overview locations: 01 main castle rear north, 02 west terrace town, 03 east harbor town, 04 south sea gate, 05 inner harbor, 06 east side waterway. Legend in Traditional Chinese: "01 主堡高台", "02 西側階梯街區", "03 東側港鎮", "04 海門", "05 內港", "06 側水道". Dimension notes only "入口淨寬 20m", "淨高 14m", "內港迴轉 40m", "側水道 14m". Footer small "比例與淨空為建模目標，施工前以船隻碰撞驗證". Use elegant crisp diagram typography. Do not draw engineering precision that cannot be known; concept illustration with explicit target dimensions. This sheet must be attractive but achievable by a small procedural/modular Three.js low-poly environment pipeline.
